# Bio-and-Resume

Professional Profile: Dave King, Ph.D.

<b>Executive Leadership & Academic Foundation</b>

My career began in academia after earning a Ph.D. in Sociology from UNC Chapel Hill, where I specialized in statistics, advanced quantitative methods, and mathematical sociology. In 1982, I transitioned to the private sector by joining Execucom, a pioneering firm in corporate financial modeling and Decision Support Systems (DSS).
Over the following 30 years, I served as a Senior and C-Level executive leading R&D, product development and product management for Comshare and JDA Software (now Blue Yonder) in the retail and supply chain management and planning sectors.  My work focused on bridging complex mathematical theory with scalable software execution, overseeing the launch of sophisticated planning and execution platforms.

<b>Technical Mastery</b>

Throughout my tenure in leadership, I remained deeply "hands-on," cultivating a vast technical repertoire. My expertise spans:

<ul>
<li>AI & Data Science: Machine Learning, NLP, Image Processing, and Data Visualization.</li>
<li>Infrastructure: Cloud Computing, IoT, and Database Architecture (SQL/NoSQL).</li>
<li>Software Engineering: Proficiency in over 65 programming languages and various operating systems.</li>
</ul>

During that time, I also co-authored a leading textbook in E-Commerce which went through 18 editions from 1999 to 2018 (see E-Commerce: A Social and Managerial Perspective), as well as a number articles in peer reviewed journals.

<b>Current Research & Advisory Work (2014–Present)</b>

Since officially retiring in 2014, I have shifted my focus toward research, strategic advisory, and the intersection of technology and art. For the past seven years, I have served as an advisor and occasional PI for an ASU startup ExSight AI (formerly Teuvonet Technologies). ExSight AI specializes in Explainable AI (XAI), developing real-time, on-board automated analysis for satellite, drone, and CCTV imagery for both commercial and defense applications. Outside of my advisory work, I continue to explore Data Science (social media analytics) and Algorithmic/Generative (Installation) Art.
